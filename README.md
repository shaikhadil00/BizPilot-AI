                                                             📌 Overview

**BizPilot AI** is an **Enterprise AI Business Intelligence Copilot** that enables users to upload business documents and interact with them using natural language.

The application leverages **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** to provide accurate, document-aware responses by combining semantic search with a powerful Large Language Model.

Whether it's company reports, financial documents, contracts, or datasets, BizPilot AI transforms static documents into an intelligent conversational assistant.

# 📖 How to Use

Using **BizPilot AI** is simple. Follow the steps below to unlock AI-powered document intelligence and business analytics.

---

# Step 1 — Upload Documents

Click **Browse Files** or drag and drop your files into the upload area.

### Supported File Types

- 📄 PDF
- 📝 DOCX
- 📊 CSV

# Step 2 — Create Knowledge Base

After uploading your files, click **Create Knowledge Base**.

BizPilot AI will automatically:

- Load all uploaded documents
- Split document content into semantic chunks
- Generate AI embeddings
- Store embeddings in ChromaDB
- Build an intelligent Knowledge Base

Once completed, the application displays:

✅ **Knowledge Base Ready**

# Step 3 — Verify System Status

The dashboard displays live status metrics.

- 🧠 Knowledge Base Status
- 📂 Total Uploaded Documents
- 💬 Total Chat Messages

This allows you to verify that the AI is ready before asking questions.

After selecting a CSV file, open the **AI Business Insights** section.

# 💡 Tips for Best Results

✔ Upload clear and readable documents.

✔ Wait until the Knowledge Base status shows **Ready** before asking questions.

✔ Upload related documents together for better contextual understanding.

✔ Use descriptive prompts instead of one-word questions.

✔ For CSV analysis, ensure the dataset contains proper column headers.

✔ Larger files may require a few extra seconds for indexing.

- CSV files unlock additional analytics and AI-powered business insights.
- All document processing occurs during runtime.
- Performance depends on document size and internet connectivity.
